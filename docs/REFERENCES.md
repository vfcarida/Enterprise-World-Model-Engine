## 2. Domain-Grouped Notes & Scope Boundaries

### 2.1 Structural Causal Models & Identification Scope
- **Richens & Everitt (2024, `arXiv:2402.10877`)** proves that an agent capable of optimal policy selection across arbitrary distributional shifts must encode an internal representation isomorphic to the environment's Structural Causal Model (SCM). In EWM, this theorem motivates the separation of structural mechanisms (`StructuralCausalModel`) from observational correlations (`EvidenceLevel`). **Scope boundary**: The mathematical theorem does not automatically validate a user-declared `CausalGraph`; real-world causal claims still require domain validation or identifiability checks (`check_backdoor_identifiability`, `check_frontdoor_identifiability`, `check_id_algorithm`).
- **VanderWeele & Ding (2017, DOI: `10.7326/M16-2607`) vs. Grünwald et al. (2024, DOI: `10.1093/jrsssb/qkae011`)**: EWM strictly separates these two concepts that share the name "E-value" in different subfields:
  - **Confounding Sensitivity E-Value** (`compute_sensitivity_e_value` in `ewm_engine.experimental.causal`): Evaluates observational sensitivity on the risk-ratio scale ($E_{\text{sens}} = RR + \sqrt{RR(RR-1)}$).
  - **Safe-Testing e-Process** (`SafeTestingEProcess` in `ewm_engine.evaluation.uncertainty`): Evaluates sequential Monte Carlo evidence under optional stopping ($\mathbb{E}_{H_0}[e_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}] \le 1$).

### 2.2 Grey-Box Conservation & Numerical Precision Scope
- **Liu & Tegmark (2021, DOI: `10.1103/PhysRevLett.126.180604`)** and **Brunton et al. (2016, DOI: `10.1073/pnas.1517384113`)** motivate the grey-box conservation layer (`ConservationLayer`, `ProjectiveConservationWrapper`) and symbolic dynamics discovery (`SINDy`). **Scope boundary**: EWM enforces linear conservation invariants via SVD nullspace projection ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{null}} = I - A^+ A$) and boundary clamping within **IEEE-754 double-precision tolerance** ($\le 10^{-12}$, observed max residual $4.55 \times 10^{-13}$ on the 80-step SEIR benchmark), not symbolic rational arithmetic.

### 2.3 Calibration & Uncertainty Quantification Scope
- **Lemos et al. (2023, `arXiv:2302.03026`, TARP)** and **Angelopoulos & Bates (2023, `arXiv:2107.07511`)** underpin EWM's empirical coverage diagnostics. **Scope boundary**: Split-conformal prediction (`SplitConformalInterval`) provides finite-sample **marginal** coverage under exchangeability, not distribution-free conditional coverage $\mathbb{P}(Y \in C(X) \mid X=x)$. Under regime shift, coverage can degrade below $1-\alpha$ unless paired with online recalibration (`AdaptiveConformalInference`, Gibbs & Candès 2021) or structural shift recovery.
