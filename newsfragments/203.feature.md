Implement decision-focused scenario discovery and multi-robustness adjudication (`ewm decide`, ADR-041, FEAT-009): introduce native Patient Rule Induction Method (PRIM) and CART to mine ensemble rollouts for interpretable vulnerability boxes; implement multi-metric robustness evaluation (Min-Max Regret, CVaR(90) Regret, Starr's Satisficing) with explicit epistemic refusal to rank when criteria disagree (McPhail et al., 2018); provide Active Inference Expected Free Energy (EFE) action scoring balancing pragmatic utility with epistemic information gain; and introduce the `ewm decide` CLI command emitting multi-surface decision reports.
