\begin{abstract}
Modern machine learning research in ``world models'' has focused heavily on continuous control, video generation, and latent-space imagination for robotics and games. Concurrently, enterprise organizations demand simulation tools to answer prescriptive interventional questions: \emph{``What would happen if we enact decision $a$ instead of baseline $a'$?''}

When applied to organizational and socio-technical domains (such as supply chain resilience, capital allocation, and crisis logistics), unconstrained neural and generative world models suffer from critical failure modes: they violate fundamental physical and accounting conservation laws, compound autoregressive rollout errors, and emit unjustified point forecasts.

In this paper, we introduce the \textbf{Enterprise World Model Engine (EWM)}, an open-source, domain-neutral structural simulation kernel. EWM establishes an architectural firewall between \textbf{KNOWN explicit conservation laws} (enforced as hard algebraic invariants) and \textbf{LEARNED probabilistic dynamics} (restricted to conservative nullspace projections). We establish the theoretical license for this architecture via Richens \& Everitt's Causal World Model Theorem (ICLR 2024), showing that robust interventional planning requires structural causal models (SCMs). EWM operationalizes this via: (1) a native SCM kernel supporting Pearl's three-step counterfactual algorithm under Twin-Rollout exogenous noise coupling; (2) automated world-model induction from relational process logs with a fail-closed causal refusal gate; (3) amortized Simulation-Based Inference (SBI) with certified coverage evaluated through the Test of Accuracy of Related Probabilities (TARP); (4) Structure-of-Arrays (SoA) state layouts producing bit-identical SHA-256 state fingerprints across pure-Python, NumPy, and compiled JAX runtimes; and (5) the World Model Interchange Format (WMIF) standard and standalone conformance suite. Across seven empirical validation benchmarks, EWM achieves bit-for-bit determinism, zero mass-balance drift ($0.000000$), and certified calibration without mandatory GPU or LLM dependencies.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
In recent years, the paradigm of world models \cite{hafner2023dreamerv3, assran2025vjepa2} has emerged as an active frontier in artificial intelligence. However, applying unconstrained generative world models to enterprise decision systems reveals acute hazards: physical and accounting quantities are hallucinated, point forecasts mask solvency tail risks, and observational associations collapse under policy shifts \cite{pearl2009causality}. We propose \textbf{Structural World Models (SWM)} to address these limitations.

\section{Theoretical License}

\newtheorem{theorem}{Theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Causal World Model Theorem]
Any agent capable of achieving robust optimal performance across an arbitrary family of distributional shifts must learn a world model that is structurally causal.
\end{theorem}
Observational models cannot escape unbounded regret under policy interventions without modeling the structural DAG $\mathcal{G}$ and the interventional $do$-operator \cite{pearl2009causality}.

\section{Architecture: Decoupling KNOWN Rules from LEARNED Dynamics}
EWM establishes the decomposition:
\begin{equation}
\Delta \mathbf{s}_t = f_{\text{known}}(\mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t) + \mathcal{P}_{\text{null}}\left( g_\theta(\mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t) \right)
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{P}_{\text{null}}(\mathbf{v}) = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}}^+ \mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}})\mathbf{v}$ projects learned dynamics onto the exact nullspace of conservation matrix $\mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}} \Delta \mathbf{s} = \mathbf{0}$.

\subsection{Twin-Rollout Counterfactual Kernel}
EWM implements Pearl's three-step algorithm: Abduction of noise $\mathbf{U}$, Action via graph surgery $do(\mathbf{a}_t = \mathbf{a}')$, and Prediction under coupled noise $\mathbf{U}$. The net treatment effect $\Delta \tau = \tau_{\text{cf}} - \tau_{\text{fact}}$ is strictly isolated from random sampling noise divergence.

\section{World-Model Induction with Honest Refusal Gate}
EWM discovers transition topologies from event logs (OCEL 2.0) and validates structural identifiability (backdoor/frontdoor). When unobserved confounding threatens validity, or covariate overlap fails ($d \ge 5$) \cite{damour2021overlap}, the engine raises a fail-closed exception rather than emitting misleading estimates. Accepted claims are paired with VanderWeele \& Ding E-values \cite{vanderweele2017evalues} and audited via refutation testing.

\section{Amortized Calibration with Certified Coverage}
Parameters are calibrated via likelihood-free Simulation-Based Inference \cite{papamakarios2019sequential}. Credible coverage is certified using the TARP diagnostic \cite{lemos2023tarp}:
\begin{equation}
P(\boldsymbol{\theta}^* \in \mathcal{R}_\alpha(\mathbf{x})) = \alpha \quad \forall \alpha \in [0, 1]
\end{equation}
Hypothesis comparisons between scenarios report sequential E-values \cite{grunwald2024evalues} satisfying $\mathbb{E}_{H_0}[E] \le 1$.

\section{Cross-Backend Bit-Identical Fingerprints \& WMIF Standard}
To enable scale without compromising determinism, EWM introduces a Structure-of-Arrays (SoA) state tensor producing bit-identical SHA-256 state fingerprints across pure-Python, NumPy, and compiled JAX backends. The World Model Interchange Format (WMIF) standardizes model exchange with an executable conformance suite.
